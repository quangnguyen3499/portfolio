## AI-Driven Automation

Automating repetitive tasks is useful, but combining automation with AI makes workflows more flexible. Large language models can read, categorize, summarize, and respond to unstructured text inside visual workflow tools like n8n.

## Sample Integration Scenarios

- Support ticket triage: classify sentiment and urgency before routing messages to the right channel.
- Resume screening: extract skills, compare them with job requirements, and save structured scores.
- Invoice audits: extract totals from documents and flag mismatches against purchase orders.

## Key Takeaways

Visual orchestration tools combined with AI let teams prototype complex workflows quickly while reducing repetitive manual work.
